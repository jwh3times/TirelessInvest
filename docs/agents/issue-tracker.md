## Board fields

- `Status`: `Todo` / `In Progress` / `Done`. Closed issues move to `Done` on their own.
- `Priority`: `P0`, the critical path of the current phase; `P1`, required before the phase ends;
  `P2`, next, and quality fixes; `P3`, later, ideas and research.
- `Gate`: why an item can or can't be picked up now. `Ready`; `Owner decision` or `Owner action`,
  which only the maintainer can move; `Needs a spec`, a design session (`/grill-with-docs` or
  `/to-spec`) first; `Research needed`; and `Depends on another issue`, `External wait` or
  `Upstream`, which go with the `blocked` label.
